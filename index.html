QUESTIONS CLIENTS USUALLY ASK.

Can YRD handle design only?

Yes. Your project can stop at the approved design and technical package or continue into fit-out and turnkey delivery.

Can you work with drawings prepared by another consultant?

Yes. We can review the existing package, identify coordination gaps and define the appropriate next stage.

Can YRD execute a design produced by another company?

Yes, subject to a technical review of the drawings, specifications and BOQ.

Do I need drawings before contacting YRD?

No. Project area, location, project type and a short description are enough for an initial review.

Can you redesign an existing or occupied office?

Yes. The project brief can consider current operations, phased works and areas that need to remain active.

What locations do you serve?

YRD is based in Cairo and handles projects across Cairo, Giza, New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, 6 October and other locations depending on project scope.
